## Reading Practice

**Daily limit:** 3 blogs max. Quality > quantity.

**Primary sources:**
- Andrej Karpathy — https://karpathy.ai/blog/
- Distill.pub — https://distill.pub/
- Gwern — https://gwern.net/
- Papers We Love — https://paperswelove.org/
- Eugene Yan — https://eugeneyan.com/
- Anthropic Research — https://www.anthropic.com/research

**The Protocol:**
1. Read one complete essay/blog post
2. Summarize the core insight in your own words
3. **Update this log immediately** — no exceptions
4. Commit and push
